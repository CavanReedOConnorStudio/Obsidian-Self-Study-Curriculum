
## [[Minor VIII]] — [[Astrology]]

A stranger inclusion than the others, and for that reason easy to underrate — but [[Astrology]] and [[cosmology]] shaped major works directly, from the astrological [[frescoes]] of the [[Palazzo Schifanoia]] to [[the Neoplatonic cosmology]] behind Botticelli, the [[alchemical]] symbolism running through [[Northern Renaissance painting]], and the [[theosophical colour-systems]] behind Kandinsky and [[Hilma af Klint]].
