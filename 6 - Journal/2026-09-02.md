- La Maison des Locataires

Humanist photographic montage

Created in 1962 by legendary French photographer [Robert Doisneau](https://www.robert-doisneau.com/en/robert-doisneau/portfolios/), _**La Maison des Locataires**_ (often translated as _The House of Tenants_ or _The Tenement House_) is a brilliant gelatin-silver photographic montage that acts as a visual cousin to Georges Perec’s literary cross-sections. Instead of drawing or painting an imaginary building, Doisneau physically constructed one using cardboard, scissors, glue, and individual prints of real people captured in their private habitats.
