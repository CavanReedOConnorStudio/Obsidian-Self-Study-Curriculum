
*But it lacks catachresis* 
*And the words drain to meaning*
*And i fear I won’t feel it* 

Thesis - Keaton Henson

Catachresis - the intentional use of a word in a incorrect, strained, or unexpected way, often to create a unique metaphor

When a text or a person's speech **lacks catachresis**, it means the language is strictly literal, standard, or conventional.
