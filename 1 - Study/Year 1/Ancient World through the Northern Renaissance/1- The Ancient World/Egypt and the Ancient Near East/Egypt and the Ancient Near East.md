## Week 2 — [[Egyptian Religion, Architecture and Image]]


By the end of this month, you should be able to understand:

## The Egyptian worldview

That Egyptian civilisation was built around the concept of **Ma'at** — the balance, order, and continuity of the universe.

You should understand that:

- The Nile shaped their understanding of cycles, renewal, and time.
- The Pharaoh was not simply a ruler but a religious and cosmic figure.
- Death was not an ending but a transition into another state of existence.
- Images were not decoration; they were active participants in maintaining order.

---

## The Egyptian approach to images

You should be able to explain:

Why Egyptian artists used:

- profile heads
- frontal eyes
- composite viewpoints
- hierarchical scale
- repeated formulas
- idealised bodies

Not as a failure to achieve realism, but because their purpose was different.

The question was not:

> "How does something appear at one moment?"

The question was:

> "What information must exist for something to continue eternally?"

---

## The connection between all disciplines

By the end, you should see:

**Geography**  
→ The Nile creates a civilisation based on cycles and renewal.

↓

**Religion**  
→ Cycles become ideas of death and rebirth.

↓

**Architecture**  
→ Tombs and temples become permanent cosmic structures.

↓

**Writing**  
→ Hieroglyphs preserve meaning beyond human life.

↓

**Art**  
→ Images become eternal representations rather than temporary observations.
