Pentimento is an Italian word meaning "repentance" or "to repent." In art, it describes the visible trace of an earlier, changed image, line, or brushstroke that an artist painted over. These ghostly layers can reappear as oil paint ages and becomes transparent over time. [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentimento), [2](https://www.britannica.com/art/pentimento-oil-painting), [3](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArtHistory/comments/1jlo949/what_is_pentimento/)]
